N° 01 / The story

Made with intention.

A corporate identity for a medical center specializing in internal medicine and disease treatment. The logo draws on the initial B and its Persian counterpart, paired with dark blue and gold to express professionalism and trust. The identity extends across stationery, folders, and printed communications.
