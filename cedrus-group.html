Discipline

Year

N° 01 / The story

Made with intention.

Menus, corporate stationery, and a wedding venue catalog for Cedrus, a hospitality complex with a restaurant, café, and wedding halls. Inspired by the cultural significance of the cypress tree, the designs bring traditional Iranian references into an elegant visual system. The printed pieces balance a sense of occasion with clarity and simplicity.
