N° 03 / About Arefeh

A thoughtful eye.
A curious mind.

I’m Arefeh Hokmabadi, a designer and researcher specializing in pattern design, branding, and visual communication.

Designer & researcher

Ideas, made tangible.

I hold a master’s degree in Visual Communication and bring over ten years of experience across branding, visual communication, and art research.

My work combines meticulous attention to detail with an interest in pattern, culture, and the ways visual ideas connect with people. From packaging and identities to catalogs and social media campaigns, I develop designs that help each brand tell its story.

I continue to refine my skills and explore new approaches, with a particular interest in brand marketing and visuals for social media.
